# Isle of Souls: pets plan

Scoped 2026-11-25. Goal: pets as close to OSRS as possible. Nothing is built yet.

## Decisions (from the user)

- **Every OSRS pet** is wired as a follower, with all of its forms. Drop sources exist only for
  content that is already built; the rest can be summoned with a dev command (`::pet <name>`).
- **Skilling pets** use the wiki formula (per-activity base chance, lowered by the current level,
  15 times more likely at 200m XP) and are hooked into each built skill, colour variants included.
- **Odds** are the real OSRS rates by default. A `petOdds` multiplier in `event-settings.json` can
  raise them on the day.
- **Full Talk-to dialogue** for every pet from the wiki transcripts, with the right chatheads, all
  Metamorphosis forms, and **Probita** on the isle to give back lost pets.

## What exists now

- Pets are plain items. The drop tables (`osrs.toml`) roll them as tertiaries and they drop like
  any other loot. Raids, the Fight Caves, the Inferno, the Colosseum and the Gauntlet give them
  through their own reward code. `event/LeaguePets` only exists so the announcer can broadcast them.
- Engine hooks with nothing behind them: the npc-type `follower` flag (cache opcode 122),
  `RegionRegistry.deleteNpc` keeping followers when an instance is torn down, the `follower_npc`
  varp (447), the `followerops_deprioritised` varbit, and the equipment tab's "Call follower"
  button (it says "You do not have a follower.").
- The cache has the follower npcs (`snake_pet_*`, `vetionpet`/`vetionpet_2`, `skillpethunter_*`,
  `phoenix_pet_*`, `poh_*` menagerie copies, and so on) and the pet objs.

## Build steps

1. **Pet data:** one table pairing every pet obj with its follower npc(s), its metamorph cycle,
   its chathead and its source. Check first whether a cache enum/struct already links them (the
   collection log and the POH menagerie must get this from somewhere). Otherwise build the table
   from the wiki and check it with a test against the cache.
2. **The follower engine:** spawn, follow (stay behind the player, no blocking), catch up after a
   teleport, region change or team-instance entry, respawn on login and despawn on logout (saved
   on the player), the `follower_npc` varp in the client's format (decode it from the client
   source), options shown only to the owner, the deprioritise setting, and Call follower.
3. **Getting a pet:** one routine `Pets.award(player, pet)` with the OSRS rules. With no follower
   out the pet follows; with space free it goes to the inventory; otherwise it is lost to Probita.
   Owning it already (follower, inventory, bank or Probita) makes it a duplicate. It also handles
   the messages, the collection log and the announcement. Every existing source is moved to this
   routine, and the drop tables stop dropping pets on the ground.
4. **The item and the npc:** Drop summons ("You already have a follower." when one is out),
   Pick-up (animation, inventory full check), Talk-to, and Metamorphosis plus item-on-pet
   recolours where OSRS has them. Pets are untradeable, can be banked, and follow the wiki's death
   rules for a following pet versus one in the inventory.
5. **Probita:** the npc and her reclaim interface or dialogue (wiki fee and rules), placed in the
   lobby.
6. **Skilling pets:** wiki base chances per activity for mining, woodcutting, fishing, runecraft,
   hunter, agility (Brimhaven arena included) and thieving, plus their variants (rock golem by ore,
   rift guardian by altar, baby chinchompa by type, and so on).
7. **Odds:** check every built boss against the wiki (some depend on kill count, level, or team
   size), add the `petOdds` setting, and keep pets unboosted by the drop-rate relics.
8. **Dialogue:** every pet's Talk-to conversation from the wiki transcripts. This is the largest
   step and can be written in batches.
9. **Tests, wiki page, client checklist.**

## Check before building

- Wiki wording of the three messages, the duplicate rule, and whether a duplicate still counts in
  the collection log.
- Death rules for a following pet and for a pet in the inventory, in safe and dangerous areas.
- Probita's current rules (free reclaim versus fee, which losses she covers).
- The `follower_npc` varp format, and how the client chooses owner-only options.

## As built (2026-11-25)

All nine steps are built in `content/areas/misc/isle-of-souls/.../pets/`, with small hooks in the
api and skill modules. Nothing has been seen in a real client yet.

### Files

- `PetCatalog.kt` (generated by `tools/pets/gen_pets.py` from the cache): 65 pets in the order of
  enum 985, 168 looks (item + follower npc + the npc's options). The script checks every item and
  that every npc has the cache's follower flag. `tools/pets/cachepets.py` dumps the cache data it
  reads (`tools/pets/cache_pets.json`).
- `Pets.kt`: the award routine, ownership, insurance bits, followers (spawn, follow, catch up,
  hide, call, transform, save/restore), odds and Probita's reclaim.
- `PetScript.kt`: Drop / Pick-up / Talk-to / Metamorphosis / Locking / Emote, items used on pets
  (following or in the inventory), item "Metamorphosis" (beaver, phoenix), login/logout, Call
  follower.
- `PetMetamorph.kt`: every family's metamorphosis rule and unlock (`PetUnlock` bits).
- `PetTalk.kt` + `resources/.../pets/dialogue.json` (generated by `tools/pets/gen_dialogue.py`
  from the 65 wiki transcripts): every look's Talk-to conversation with its menus, random picks
  and conditions.
- `Probita.kt`: Probita in the Soul Wars lobby (2210, 2841), her transcript dialogue, the pet
  insurance screen (interface 148) with Reclaim and Confirm.
- `PetDropScript.kt`: drop-table pets are claimed off the floor and awarded; their chance follows
  `petOdds`.
- `SkillingPets.kt`: the wiki formula for `SkillingPetRoll` events; rift guardian colours.
- `PetCommands.kt`: `::pet <key or name> [look]`, `::pets`, `::petunlock <name|all>`,
  `::petreset`.
- Wiki: `wiki/pets.html` (data from `tools/wiki/gen_pets.py`), linked from every page.

### Engine and api changes

- `NpcDropEvents.Modify.chanceMultipliers` and a matching `DropRoller.roll` parameter: an item's
  own chance factor (pets use `petOdds`, never the relics' boost). NpcDeath, Scurrius, the
  wilderness bosses and the giants pass it on.
- Scurrius, wilderness boss and giant loot now publish `NpcDropEvents.Claim` like `NpcDeath`, so
  their pets are claimed too.
- `api/player`: `SkillingPetRoll` (published by mining, woodcutting, fishing, hunter
  chinchompas, thieving pickpockets and stalls, the Brimhaven arena tickets and runecraft) and
  `FollowerCall` (the equipment tab's Call follower button publishes it).
- Every other pet source goes through `Pets.award`: Fight Caves, Inferno (and the cape trade-in,
  refused while the pet is owned), Colosseum (and the quiver gamble), Gauntlet (always the plain
  Youngllef, the Corrupted Gauntlet unlocks the corrupted look), ToA / ToB / CoX (the pet is
  awarded when it is taken out of the reward chest).
- The announcer no longer announces drop-table pets itself: `Pets.award` announces following,
  backpack and duplicate/lost pets with their own lines.

### Saved state (new varps, need `packCache`)

`isle_pet_follower` (65167), `isle_pet_insured_1..3` (65166-65164), `isle_pet_unlocks` (65163),
`isle_pet_rift_unlocks` (65162). The client's `follower_npc` (447) gets the follower's npc index,
which is what makes its options show for the owner only.

### Decisions and guesses

- Pets on the collection log's "All Pets" page of the rev 233 cache are "every OSRS pet" (65).
  Pets added to OSRS after rev 233 (Gull, Soup, Orion, ...) do not exist in the cache. Cats, dogs
  and other generic followers are not part of it.
- Deaths never take items on the isle, so pets are never lost by dying (OSRS loses inventory pets
  on an unsafe death; Probita would give them back).
- Guessed messages: the lost-pet line (same as the duplicate one), "Your <pet> doesn't seem able
  to change yet.", the Locking lines, "You need 250 of those firelighters...", the heron's
  not-enough-flakes line, "Probita hands your <pet> back to you.", the gold chinchompa warning
  text.
- Guessed: rock golem ores and Rocky's berries are not used up (the wiki only says so for seeds,
  logs and unlock items); a pet comes in the ore's / chinchompa's / altar's colour; beaver
  Metamorphosis switches between the log colour, pheasant and fox.
- Emote animations for Wisp, Butch and Yami are picked by name from the cache (Wisp's and
  Butch's death emotes need the awakened kills and are left out). The cooldown is 8 ticks.
- No facial expressions in Talk-to (the transcripts have none): every line uses the neutral one.
- Hidden-follower areas: only the Brimhaven Agility Arena. The pet's receive sound is not played
  (no synth found for it).
- Probita's spot (2210, 2841) has not been checked for walkable floor.
- Skilling chances for activities the wiki does not list use the nearest default (pickpockets
  257,211, stalls 36,490, fish 257,770, trees 317,647).
- Jad and Zuk's better odds on a Slayer task are not built (the isle cannot assign those tasks).

### Tests

`PetsTest` (13 integration tests: catalog vs cache, award outcomes, duplicates, drop/pick-up,
metamorphosis, unlock rules, Call follower, catching up after a teleport, logout/login, drop-table
claim and odds, skilling formula, rift guardian recolour). The isle's whole integration suite
passes apart from failures that also happen without these changes (Zulrah boat tile, Zulrah
scales, and the sandbox settings' shortened Inferno, Gauntlet node depletion and Fight Cave
waves).

### Client checklist

1. `./gradlew packCache`, then log in.
2. `::pet vorki`: Vorki appears and follows; its options show for you only (check with a second
   account). Walk, teleport, take a ladder: it keeps up.
3. Pick-up, then Drop it from the inventory. Call follower from 5+ tiles away.
4. Talk-to a few pets (`::pet olmlet`, `::pet rift_guardian`): the menus and chatheads look right.
5. Metamorphosis on a snakeling, Kalphite princess; `::petunlock all` then an olmlet and Tumeken's
   guardian. Use an ore on a rock golem, a seed on a tangleroot, logs on a beaver after a funky
   shaped log.
6. With a pet out and a full inventory, `::pet scurry`: lost. Talk to Probita in the lobby, open the
   insurance screen, pick it, Confirm: it comes back. Check her spot is on walkable floor.
7. Enter the Brimhaven Agility Arena with a pet: "Your pet has been hidden...", it returns outside.
8. Log out and in with a pet out: it comes back.
9. Wisp / Butch / Yami Emote: the animations play.
